Opening hours: We are open from 8am to 6pm, Tuesday to Sunday. We are closed on Mondays.

Location: 12 Jalan Bukit Bintang, Kuala Lumpur. The nearest LRT station is Bukit Bintang, a 5-minute walk.

Parking: We do not have our own parking. Public parking is available at Lot 10 car park, 2 minutes away, RM3 per hour.

Menu - Drinks: Latte RM12, Americano RM9, Teh Tarik RM6, Iced Chocolate RM13.

Menu - Food: Nasi Lemak RM10, Croissant RM8, Chicken Sandwich RM14, Banana Cake RM9.

Vegan options: Our Americano, Iced Chocolate (made with oat milk on request), and Banana Cake are vegan-friendly. The Croissant contains butter and is not vegan.

Reservations: We accept reservations for groups of 6 or more. Call 03-1234-5678 at least one day ahead.

Payment: We accept cash, credit cards, and TNG eWallet. We do not accept cheques.

Wi-Fi: Free Wi-Fi is available. Ask the cashier for the password.
